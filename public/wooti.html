04The Four Platforms

Each has its own rhythm. Don't cross-post identical content. Native uploads every time, no watermarks.

LinkedIn

Priority 1
Cadence: 2-3 posts/week per brand page + 3-5 personal posts/week + 7 warm comments/day + 10 DMs/day
  • Company pages: Systems + Bookings. Case studies, wins, screenshots, behind the scenes.
  • Your personal profile: you post as yourself. Tag Sovereign. This reaches 10x further than company-page-only.
  • Warm comments first: comment value on target posts for 5-7 days BEFORE DMing. No "great post".
  • Long-form articles: one deep post every 2-3 weeks. Algorithm rewards it.
Bookings page Systems live site (for screenshots)

TikTok

Priority 1
Cadence: Daily. Bookings-first. Garden room content.
  • Format: face-to-camera OR voice-over AI visuals. Raw, not polished.
  • Angles: PPA explainer, "why most garden room sites lose money", before/after walkthroughs (Tranquil), build speed, AI leverage.
  • No watermarks: export clean from your editor, upload native.
  • Clickable link unlocks at 1k followers. Until then, put the URL in plain text in bio.
Tranquil walkthrough source

Instagram

Priority 2
Cadence: 1 reel/day + 3 stories/day + DMs
  • Reels: alternate brand focus, lean Bookings 70%.
  • Stories: behind the scenes, polls, countdowns. Keeps the algorithm warm.
  • DMs: after warm engagement. Voice notes land better than text.
  • Bio link: point at sovereignbookings.co.uk for Bookings, sovereignsystem.co.uk for Systems.

Facebook

Priority 2
Cadence: 3 posts/week per brand + 5 groups
  • Group strategy: join 5 local trade groups (NW England), post value once a week each.
  • Page posts: case studies, client wins, content repurposed from LinkedIn.
  • Messenger: same rules as IG DMs. Warm first, never cold pitch.

07Hard Rules

Break these and we have a conversation. They exist for a reason.

Rule 01

No em dashes in any copy

Biggest AI tell on the internet. Hyphens or full stops only. Every caption, DM, post.

Rule 02

No buying followers

Grow genuinely. 3-5 real posts per week. No shortcuts, no pods, no engagement rings.

Rule 03

Never post Systems retainer pricing publicly

Sales-call only. Post "starts at £99/mo" max, never full tier breakdown.

Rule 04

Never cross brand streams

Systems offer goes on Systems channels only. Bookings offer goes on Bookings channels only. No mixing.

Rule 05

No AI-generated stock visuals

Use real Tranquil / dashboard / build-in-public assets. Veo 2, Nano Banana, phone captures allowed.

Rule 06

No emojis unless trade-relevant

Hammer, spanner, garden, hard hat = fine. Fire, rocket, hundred-points = absolutely not.

Rule 07

Week 1 is approval-gated

Loom every draft to Miller before posting. After Week 1 you've got free rein within the rules.

Rule 08

Warm before DM, always

Comment on their posts for 5-7 days before ever sliding in. Cold DMs from new accounts get reported.
