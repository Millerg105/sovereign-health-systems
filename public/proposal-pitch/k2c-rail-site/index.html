What we do

Six disciplines, one delivery model.

Every K2C engagement is led by a Chartered or Member-grade engineer. No back-office sub-contracting to graduates. No padding the timesheet. We turn up, we build the plan, we ship the asset.

01 · Programme

Programme & project management

RISQS-audited delivery on multi-disciplinary rail programmes. Stage-gate governance, earned-value reporting, integrated risk registers. NEC4 and ICE-form contracts.

02 · Preconstruction

Preconstruction & engineering management

Constructability reviews, buildability analysis, value engineering. Pre-bid technical advisory for Tier-1 contractors chasing Network Rail and TfL framework work.

03 · Design

Design management & constructability

Civils, structures, and permanent way design coordination. Common Data Environment governance. Approval-in-Principle and AIP-2 packs through Network Rail GRIP / PACE.

04 · Track

Slab track & novel track form design

Slab, embedded, and ballastless track form design and constructability. Trackbed investigation techniques published in PWI Journal. Heavy haul, high-speed, and light rail.

05 · Permanent Way

Permanent way surveying, design, renewal, maintenance

End-to-end p-way lifecycle. Site survey, renewals programming, possession planning, maintenance regimes. Tamper trials support recognised in the Balfour Beatty Global Annual Report.

06 · Systems

Systems integration

Heavy rail, high-speed rail, light rail and tram, rapid transport. Multi-system interface management for clients moving between heritage and modern signalling, traction, and PWay regimes.

Flagship technology

Engine Carbon Clean. Hydrogen-injection cleaning at fleet scale.

The methodology behind the 2025 Balfour Beatty SHELT Sustainability Award. Hydrogen-based combustion-chamber cleaning, deployed in-service across diesel locomotive fleets and plant. Independently measured reductions of 7.5% to 30% in fuel burn and CO₂ output across the first cycle.

Delivered in partnership with Advanced Hydrogen Technologies Group. The data outputs feed directly into the CP7 sustainability scoring submissions our framework partners now have to evidence under Network Rail’s Control Period 7 requirements.
