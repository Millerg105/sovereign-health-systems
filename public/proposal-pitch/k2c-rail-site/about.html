Why we founded K2C

Two brothers and one engineering director sick of watching good work get diluted.

The pattern across the industry by 2023 was clear. Big consultancies winning frameworks on Tier-1 credentials, then dropping the delivery onto graduates with no permanent way background. Tier-1 contractors paying for board-level advisory and getting mid-bench output.

K2C is the response. Three engineers, all Chartered or Member-grade. Founder-led delivery on every job. Fellows of the Permanent Way Institution at the technical lead position, not the marketing brochure.

Founded May 2024. First award October 2025. PWI Journal publication December 2025. Recognised in Balfour Beatty’s Global Annual Report inside eighteen months of trading.

Operating principles
  • Founder-led. One of the three names above is on every project. No exceptions.
  • Chartered or Member-grade only. Lead engineer on any package holds CEng, MCIOB, MPWI, NECReg, or equivalent.
  • Tell the client the truth. If a scope doesn’t need what was tendered, we say so before the contract.
  • Specification first. Network Rail standards, RISQS audit posture, NEC4 form. We don’t freelance with regulated environments.
  • Stand behind the asset. If something we built fails in service, we fix it. That sits in our terms.
