Services

Six disciplines, one delivery model.

Every K2C service area is led by a Chartered or Member-grade engineer. We take packages that match the team’s competence and partner-in for the rest. No padding the bid with credentials we can’t evidence.

Heavy rail High-speed rail Light rail & tram Rapid transit & metro
01
Programme & project management

Multi-disciplinary rail programmes delivered to stage gate.

RISQS-audited delivery on Tier-1 frameworks. Programme build-up, earned-value reporting, integrated risk registers, NEC4 contract administration. Stage-gate governance against Network Rail GRIP and PACE, TfL Project Development Manual, Metrolinx M1-04.

Suited to clients running a complex package across multiple disciplines where coordination is the bottleneck, not engineering capacity.

Typical deliverables
  • Programme baseline and target-cost model under NEC4 ECC
  • Stage-gate dossiers for GRIP / PACE / TfL approvals
  • Risk-and-opportunity register with quantified contingency
  • Earned-value monthly reporting to client and finance
  • Supply-chain governance across Tier-2 and specialist sub-contractors
02
Preconstruction & engineering management

The work that decides whether a programme can actually be built.

Constructability reviews, value engineering, buildability analysis. Pre-bid technical advisory for Tier-1 contractors chasing Network Rail and TfL framework work. We sit on the bid team, not in a back room.

This is where the worst-cost overruns get prevented or baked in. We turn up early and pick the build approach the rest of the programme depends on.

Typical deliverables
  • Constructability and buildability reports
  • Value engineering studies with quantified savings
  • Pre-bid technical advisory for Tier-1 frameworks
  • Possession planning and Form C-style application support
  • Method statement and SSOW package development
03
Design management & constructability

Civils, structures and permanent way coordinated as one design.

Common Data Environment governance. Approval-in-Principle and AIP-2 packs through Network Rail PACE. BIM-coordinated multi-disciplinary design management. Interface management between Tier-1 design partners and the client’s engineering authority.

Typical deliverables
  • AIP-1 / AIP-2 packs through Network Rail PACE
  • CDE governance across multi-disciplinary teams
  • Interface registers for civils / track / signalling / OLE
  • Design assurance evidence packs for IDC and IDR
  • Design risk management under CDM 2015
04
Slab track & novel track form design

Ballastless, embedded, novel. Designed for the next fifty years of service.

Slab, embedded, and ballastless track form design and constructability. Trackbed investigation techniques published in PWI Journal. Heavy haul, high-speed, and light rail. The methodology we developed with Sol Solution and SNCF is now the reference our partners specify against.

Typical deliverables
  • Slab track design and constructability advice
  • Trackbed investigation methodology (PWI Journal published)
  • Embedded rail design for light rail and tram
  • Transition design between ballasted and ballastless
  • Whole-life cost modelling per track form option
05
Permanent way

Survey, design, renewal, maintenance. End-to-end p-way lifecycle.

Site survey, renewals programming, possession planning, maintenance regimes. Tamper trials support recognised in the Balfour Beatty Global Annual Report. Track quality regression analysis, ride-quality remediation, defect classification, S&C renewals.

Typical deliverables
  • P-way condition survey and asset register
  • Renewals programme against route asset management strategy
  • Possession packs and engineering acceptance evidence
  • Tamper trials support and post-trial validation
  • Defect classification and remediation methodology
06
Systems integration

Heavy rail, high-speed, light rail, metro. Interfaces managed across the stack.

Multi-system interface management for clients moving between heritage and modern signalling, traction, and PWay regimes. Particularly suited to renewals and re-signalling programmes where the interface count is the principal risk.

Typical deliverables
  • System interface register and management
  • Heritage-to-modern transition planning
  • Signalling, traction, PWay coordination
  • Integrated test & commissioning support
  • Operational acceptance evidence packs
Engagement models

Three ways K2C plugs in.

Model 01

Embedded technical lead

K2C engineer embedded on the client’s team for the duration of a programme. CDir or CEng-grade lead, day-rate or fixed-term basis.

Best for: Tier-1 contractors winning framework work who need permanent way authority for delivery.

Model 02

Package delivery

K2C takes a discrete package end-to-end. Programme management, design management, or technical assurance. Fixed scope, NEC4 form, milestone-linked invoicing.

Best for: Tier-1 contractors and infrastructure owners with a defined deliverable and a fixed budget.

Model 03

Joint venture & partnership

K2C joins a bid consortium or partnership as the rail engineering capability. Tier-2 contractors winning above their solo grade. Tier-1 framework partners filling a permanent way gap.

Best for: Bid consortia chasing Network Rail, TfL, or Metrolinx framework lots.

Standards we work to

Regulated environments, audit-grade evidence.

Every deliverable goes through the regime that the client’s assurance team needs to evidence to their regulator. We don’t freelance the spec.
