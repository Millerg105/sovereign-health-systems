Why this matters

Pipeline visibility is the difference between bidding what fits and bidding what’s left.

Most Tier-2 and Tier-1 consultancies find frameworks the way they always have: word of mouth, late-stage Tier-1 introductions, and the announcement after it’s gone live. The K2C Pipeline Monitor turns that into a structured intelligence layer.

The advantage compounds. The earlier K2C sees a framework, the earlier the partnership conversation happens with the right Tier-1, the earlier the bid team is built, the higher the win rate.
