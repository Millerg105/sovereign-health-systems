Research & published work

K2C’s intellectual capital, in the public record.

Permanent way and rail engineering only progresses when the methodology behind each project gets published, peer-reviewed, and replicated. K2C invests in publication routes because the next bid is partly evidenced by the last paper. What follows is what we’ve put on the record so far.

PWI Journal · Published

Innovative trackbed investigation techniques.

PWI Journal · 2025 · Lead author: Niall Fagan CEng FICE FPWI

A peer-reviewed paper published in the PWI (Permanent Way Institution) Journal documenting the methodology K2C developed with Sol Solution SAS and SNCF for high-resolution trackbed condition investigation. The technique uses surface-deployed instrumentation to derive sub-ballast condition without destructive sampling, materially reducing investigation cost and possession time on heavy-haul corridors.

The paper sets out the field methodology, the calibration regime against destructive sampling baselines, and the four-route validation programme run on UK and French heavy-haul lines through 2023-2024.

Read on PWI Journal →
Key findings
  • Investigation time reduced 60-75% against destructive sampling baseline
  • Possession requirement reduced to a single mid-week window per route
  • Sub-ballast condition correlation within 8% of destructive samples across 16 calibration points
  • Methodology transferable to slab-track preconstruction surveys
  • Joint authorship with Sol Solution SAS engineering team
2025 SHELT Sustainability Award

Engine Carbon Clean field study

Recognised by Balfour Beatty in 2025 for material reduction in fleet-level carbon emissions delivered in-service across their permanent way plant. The methodology and data are now part of the evidence base feeding into CP7 sustainability scoring submissions across multiple Tier-1 contractors.

Featured in: Balfour Beatty Global Annual Report 2025

Tamper trials validation

Permanent way plant validation programme

K2C provided engineering and validation support to the tamper trials programme delivered into the Balfour Beatty permanent way fleet. The trials programme contributed to the calibration regime now used across the fleet for high-output renewal work. Recognition published in Balfour Beatty’s Global Annual Report.

Period: 2024-2025
