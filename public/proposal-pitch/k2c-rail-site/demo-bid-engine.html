How the engine works under the hood

Three layers, one engineer-led pipeline.

Layer 01

RFP ingest & clause extraction

Tender documents parsed into a structured clause register. Each scoring section, each evidence requirement, each compliance gate identified and tagged against the K2C capability matrix.

Layer 02

Past-performance auto-draft

K2C’s project history indexed against the requirement tags. For each scoring section, the engine drafts past-performance evidence directly from the project record. Engineer reviews, signs off, edits as needed.

Layer 03

Compliance scorecard

Every scoring section ranked against K2C’s evidence. Gaps surfaced for human attention. Format and word count auto-adapted to the tender’s submission template.

What the engine doesn’t do.

It doesn’t submit. It doesn’t sign off. It doesn’t make engineering claims. Every bid leaves the building under a Chartered or Member-grade engineer’s name, after that engineer has reviewed every claim. The engine compresses the writing-time tax. The judgement stays with the engineer.
