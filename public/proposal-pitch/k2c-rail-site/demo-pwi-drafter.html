Operating discipline

The drafter sits below the engineer. Not next to.

What it does

Compresses writing time.

  • Parses the project deliverable record into PWI house structure.
  • Drafts abstract, introduction, methodology paragraphs from the engineering judgement log.
  • Renders result tables in PWI Journal format.
  • Pulls citations from the K2C reference library.
  • Flags areas where the project record is thin and a human input is needed.
What it doesn’t do

Make engineering claims.

  • It doesn’t derive new conclusions from the data.
  • It doesn’t cite work it hasn’t been given as a reference.
  • It doesn’t submit. Every article leaves K2C under a Chartered or Member-grade engineer’s name.
  • It doesn’t bypass peer review. Editorial review queue is the gate, not optional.
Why this matters commercially

Publication is the next bid’s evidence.

UK rail procurement, especially at Tier-1 framework level, scores past performance partly on published thought leadership. A PWI Journal article is a credibility multiplier on the next PQQ. A consultancy that publishes one paper a year is operating at one cadence. K2C is targeting four a year.

The constraint isn’t engineering output, it’s the writing-time tax. The drafter takes that tax to near zero. The engineer still owns the judgement and the editorial process is unchanged.
