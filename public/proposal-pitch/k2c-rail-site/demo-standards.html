How the monitor works

Three signals, one alignment dashboard.

Signal 01

Standards-body change feed

Daily ingest from Network Rail, TfL, RSSB, PWI, RISQS, Metrolinx, AREMA, Transport Canada, EU TSI, Iarnród Éireann, SNCF technical regulations, ISO. Every clause change tagged and stored.

Signal 02

K2C deliverable corpus

Every K2C service description, methodology document, and project deliverable indexed against the standards corpus. Drift detected when a standard moves and the deliverable doesn’t.

Signal 03

Bid-stage alignment check

Every new PQQ goes through the monitor before submission. Any unaligned clause is flagged and the bid team is shown the gap before it lands on a scoring sheet.
