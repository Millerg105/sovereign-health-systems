Strategic Logic

UK methodology, deployed under Canadian engineering regimes.

Canadian rail infrastructure is in a renewal cycle that mirrors UK CP7 by approximately three years. Toronto’s Metrolinx GO Expansion programme, VIA Rail’s High Frequency Rail proposal, the Ontario Line, REM in Montreal. The capability needs being procured now are the capability needs K2C has been delivering in the UK since 2024.

Most UK rail consultancies that move to Canada arrive with the UK approach and try to retrofit Canadian regimes onto it. K2C is doing the opposite. The methodology stays intact, the regulatory and engineering-acceptance interface gets rebuilt for Metrolinx M1-04, AREMA, and Transport Canada.

Canadian Market Context

Where the demand is concentrated.

Metrolinx

GO Expansion programme

Ontario’s commuter rail electrification and capacity expansion. Multi-billion dollar capital programme. Permanent way renewal and electrification interface is the K2C-fit capability.

VIA Rail

High Frequency Rail

The Quebec-Toronto corridor proposal. Slab track and novel track form design directly maps to K2C’s published PWI Journal work with Sol Solution / SNCF.

Ontario Line

Toronto rapid transit

New automated metro line, multi-year construction programme. Systems integration and possession-heavy renewals capability transferable from UK Crossrail / Elizabeth Line experience base.

REM Montreal

Réseau express métropolitain

Montreal’s automated light metro. Phase 2 and operational ramp-up. Light rail systems integration and PWay maintenance regime aligned with K2C’s European light rail experience.

CN & CPKC

Class 1 freight renewals

Canadian National and Canadian Pacific Kansas City. Heavy-haul renewals programmes. Engine Carbon Clean methodology has direct application to Class 1 locomotive fleets.

Transport Canada

Regulatory interface

Federal regulator on Class 1 and intercity rail. AREMA / TC interface is the integration layer between K2C’s UK PWI methodology and Canadian engineering acceptance.
