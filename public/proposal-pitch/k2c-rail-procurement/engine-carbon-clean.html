Methodology

Four stages. No engine teardown. Audit-grade evidence.

The cleaning process runs in-service across a 60-90 minute window per unit. Hydrogen injection lifts deposited carbon from piston crowns, valve seats and injector tips. Residue exits as standard combustion product. Every reading written to the unit’s service record.

Stage 01

Baseline measurement

Fuel burn rate, exhaust opacity and turbo back-pressure measured against the unit’s service-record baseline. This is what every reduction figure is measured against.

Stage 02

Hydrogen injection cycle

Controlled hydrogen flow injected through the air intake at engine idle. Six controlled cycles across 60 to 90 minutes depending on engine displacement. Operator trackside throughout, full operational supervision.

Stage 03

Post-service validation

Repeat measurement of fuel burn, exhaust opacity and back-pressure. Delta written to the unit’s service record and uploaded to the K2C ECC dashboard for CP7 reporting evidence.

Stage 04

Quarterly re-baseline

Standard re-measurement at the next quarterly service. Most fleets see partial drift back toward baseline at 12-18 months. Scheduled cleaning becomes part of the maintenance regime.
