Core Capabilities

Six disciplines, one delivery model.

Every K2C engagement is led by a Chartered or Member-grade engineer. RISQS-audited delivery, evidenced past performance, founder accountability on every project.

01

Programme & project management

RISQS-audited delivery on multi-disciplinary rail programmes. Stage-gate governance through Network Rail GRIP / PACE and TfL PDM. NEC4 and ICE-form contracts. Earned-value reporting.

02

Preconstruction & engineering management

Constructability reviews, value engineering, pre-bid technical advisory for Tier-1 contractors. Buildability analysis on Network Rail and TfL framework work.

03

Design management & constructability

Common Data Environment governance. AIP-1 / AIP-2 packs through Network Rail PACE. Interface registers across civils, track, signalling and OLE.

04

Slab track & novel track form design

Slab, embedded and ballastless track form design. Trackbed investigation methodology published in PWI Journal with Sol Solution / SNCF. Transition design and whole-life cost modelling.

05

Permanent way

End-to-end p-way lifecycle. Site survey, renewals programming, possession planning, maintenance regimes. Tamper trials support recognised in the Balfour Beatty Global Annual Report.

06

Systems integration

Heavy rail, high-speed, light rail, metro. Interface management between heritage and modern signalling, traction and PWay regimes. Integrated test and commissioning support.

Flagship Technology

Engine Carbon Clean. Hydrogen-injection cleaning at fleet scale.

The methodology behind the 2025 Balfour Beatty Supplier SHELT Sustainability Award. Hydrogen-based combustion-chamber cleaning, deployed in-service across diesel locomotive fleets and plant. Independently measured fuel and CO₂ reductions of 7.5% to 30% across the first servicing cycle.

Delivered in partnership with Advanced Hydrogen Technologies Group. The data outputs feed directly into the CP7 sustainability scoring submissions our framework partners now have to evidence under Network Rail’s Control Period 7 requirements.
